    What is Global Warming?
Global warming is a major result of climate change. It refers to the increase in Earth's temperature due to human activities like pollution and deforestation. This rise in temperature leads to life-threatening issues such as floods, storms, and droughts. In this global warming essay in English, we look at the causes, such as industrialization and the release of greenhouse gases, which are causing this dangerous warming. The effects of global warming essay highlight how this rise in temperature is already impacting the planet.

According to recent research, the four primary contributors to global warming are: greenhouse gases, deforestation, pollution, and per capita carbon emissions.

<h2>Solution for Global Warming</h2>
To effectively address global warming, both individual actions and large-scale efforts are necessary. While industries contribute more to carbon emissions, individuals can play a key role through sustainable practices such as using hybrid cars, reducing plastic waste, and promoting reforestation. Governments also need to implement clear policies to prevent further environmental damage. Restricting deforestation and regulating industrial practices are crucial. Collective action at all levels will help mitigate the negative impacts of global warming, creating a healthier future for both the environment and humanity. This global warming essay emphasizes the importance of individual actions in combating the crisis.
